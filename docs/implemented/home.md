# App shell & Home

## Tab shell (`ui/navigation/MainScreen.kt`, SHELL-01/03)

- The Figma bar (`ShifaNavBar`): Home, Bookings, Shifa AI, Documents, Messages. The labels are localized. The plan's 4-tab list (Home, Bookings, Documents, Doctors) is older than the design; this follows Figma.
- Home, Bookings ([bookings.md](bookings.md)), Shifa AI ([shifa-ai.md](shifa-ai.md)) and Documents ([documents.md](documents.md)) are built. So are My Tasks and the check-in ([tasks.md](tasks.md)), and Notifications ([notifications.md](notifications.md)). Messages is a "Coming soon" placeholder. The Shifa AI tab is hidden when the profile shuts it (09 §7). The avatar opens Profile ([profile.md](profile.md)).
- Android back: a page closes, then a tab other than Home goes to Home, then the app closes (02 §10).
- Tabs keep their state (`rememberSaveableStateHolder`). ViewModels live in a store scoped to the signed-in session, so none outlive a logout. This is hand-rolled until the nav library (deferred) gives per-destination scoping. SHELL-02 (routes, redirect guard) is not built.
- Header (SHELL-03): `ShifaMainHeader` with the avatar (Profile) and the bell (Notifications). The bell shows the N2 unread count, refreshed after N3 or N4, capped at 9+. Figma has no badge, so it uses the Material one. The chat icon and its badge are not in the Figma header.

## Home (`ui/home/`, HOME-01..04)

- Figma: `1647:10075`. It shows the header ("Hello, {first name}", from P1; a saved Edit profile or photo change shows at once, from P2's reply), then Upcoming appointments, My Tasks and My Doctors. Each section shows up to 3 cards (10 §3.4). Upcoming is hidden when there are none. Pull to refresh reloads everything.
- Loading: P1, N2, B2 and T1 run in parallel, then D2 runs for the doctors. A list shows a spinner until it first loads and its empty text when empty. A failed load shows one error banner. A failed reload keeps the previous lists. A section whose first load failed is hidden. Failed P1 and N2 calls only cost the name and the badge. A 401 on P1 logs out. The app-wide 401 handler (CORE-05) is not built.
- Upcoming (HOME-01): one unfiltered B2 call, instead of Flutter's `status=CONFIRMED&startDate=today`. It keeps every open visit (not cancelled or completed, so REQUESTED and PENDING too: new bookings come back as either, 15 B2) until its join window closes (start − 5 min to end + 15 min, 06). Flutter dropped a visit once it started. During the window the card says "Now", and confirmed video visits show Join. An unconfirmed visit (`REQUESTED`: every new booking, until the doctor confirms it) adds "· Pending" after its time; `IN_PROGRESS` counts as confirmed, and one waiting for payment "· Payment pending" (not in Figma). The list is sorted soonest first. The section arrow shows only with more than 3 visits, like My Tasks and My Doctors. Figma hides it; it replaces Flutter's "all bookings" button and opens the Upcoming appointments page (`UpcomingScreen.kt`): every upcoming visit as a flat card, from the same list, laid out like Past (no Figma frame). Tapping a card or Join opens the appointment details.
- My Tasks (HOME-02): T1, open tasks only (not completed or cancelled). Flutter showed a static card. Each card shows the category tag and "Starts: dd.MM.yyyy" for a future start, else "Started: …" (12 §3). The cards and the arrow open My Tasks, which Home refreshes after a check-in.
- My Doctors (HOME-03): Figma replaces "Doctors near me" with "My Doctors". The list is the doctors of the 3 most recent bookings, taken from the same B2 response and loaded with D2. There's no location flow and no D1 call. A doctor that fails to load is left out. The card shows the rating as "x.x(N)", verified when there are certificates, and the next free slot as "Today · HH:mm" / "Tomorrow · HH:mm" / "dd.MM · HH:mm". The arrow opens the Bookings tab's My Doctors page with every doctor ([bookings.md](bookings.md)).
- HOME-04: Home has its own ViewModel and state, so it can't overwrite other tabs' lists.
- Professions show as the API sends them. The translated catalog comes with DOC-01.
- `sanitizeError` now applies the auth-only 401 "Invalid credentials" and 404 "No account found" texts to `/auth/*` calls only (01 §8). Elsewhere, a 404 falls back to the call's default message.
